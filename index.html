About Us

Founded in 2021, the Cambridge University Biotech Society is Cambridge's only student-led organisation dedicated to making biotech accessible to undergraduates. We connect students directly with the city's world-class ecosystem — from spinout founders and life science VCs to leading academics and industry professionals — fostering a new generation of biotech entrepreneurs.
