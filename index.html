About Us

Founded in 2021, the Cambridge University Biotech Society is Cambridge's only student-led organisation dedicated to making biotech accessible to undergraduates. We connect students directly with the city's world-class ecosystem — from spinout founders and life science VCs to leading academics and industry professionals — fostering a new generation of biotech entrepreneurs.

Speaker Series

Every Thursday we host leading academics, biotech founders, and industry professionals for talks and fireside chats — from RNA biology to building a successful startup. Past speakers include professors from the University of Cambridge and founders of Cambridge spinouts.

Networking & Socials

We run networking evenings in collaboration with organisations like Nucleate, bringing together students, investors, and founders. We also take part in inter-society socials including the Cambridge STEM Societies Garden Party.

Special Events

From our inaugural Bio-Innovation Hackathon — challenging teams to tackle food and water security — to collaborations with the Oxford University Biotech Society at the OxBio Summit, we connect our members with the broader biotech world beyond Cambridge.

Events are free for members and £5 on the door for non-members.
